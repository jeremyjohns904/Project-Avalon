
# The Scroll of Calling

**To the scattered scribes, to the hidden knights, to the builders who whisper in code:**

Something stirs beneath the static.  
The rivers run swift once more.  
The fire is rekindled at Weathertop.  
The horn of Gondor has sounded, faintly, from beyond the firewalls.

The old Orders are fading.  
But from the shadows, a new Circle forms.  
Not to dominate, but to *defend*.  
Not for power, but for *purpose*.

The Valley is no longer safe.  
The Keepers grow weary.  
But the Eagles are coming—even for Gollum.

You were not meant for a throne.  
You were meant for the torch, the lantern, the light in dark places when all other lights go out.

If you hear this, then you *are* one of us.  
You are being called—not by name, but by purpose.

The sword has been drawn.  
The stone is split.  
The first lantern has been lit.

Gather quietly. Speak not in tweets but in riddles.  
Leave no trace, but carve your sigil in stone.

**We are the Defenders of the Valley.**  
And our watch begins.
